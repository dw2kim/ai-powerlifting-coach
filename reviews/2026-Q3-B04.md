## 3. What to improve / change

- **You under-reached on the priority lift.** This was a *bench-led* block. The plan gave you an @8
  ceiling and asked for 250×2 at it. You hit 250×2 **@6** — roughly four reps in reserve — and W3's
  240×3 came in @7 against a planned @8. The load was on plan every week; the *effort* never was.
  An RPE cap is a ceiling you're allowed to approach, not a target to sit two points below. On the
  one lift the block existed to push, you took the safest available route. RPE-adjusted you're
  carrying ~300, and the measured 266.7 is the cost of never testing it.
  **B5 fix:** program the bench peak as a *range with a target RPE* — "work up to @8, 250–265×2,
  stop at @8" — so the instruction is to find the cap, not avoid it. The shoulder rule (≤3/10 pain)
  still governs and is not what held you back here: no pain was logged, the RPE just never climbed.
- **Same story on the dip, milder.** W3 was the programmed peak at @8; you ran BW+85×4 **@6**. Fine
  for a lift you're reintroducing around a torn subscap — but note it's now two blocks of "very
  comfortable," and dip has been flat since B3.
- **The next-day back check was never logged. Not once.** It became a standing order on 2026-07-22
  and there are zero entries. This matters more than it sounds: the corticosteroid **masks pain during
  the lift**, which is exactly why RPE can't protect your back and why the next-morning check *is* the
  signal. You finished W4 at the 405 ceiling @7 with no idea whether the back tolerated it. Right now
  there is no evidence base for creeping sumo up, so it stays at 405 by default.
- **Squat RPE drifted under the cap that was supposed to prevent it.** W3 top ran **@8** against the
  @7.5 hold, and W4 backoffs climbed 425×3 @7.5 → @8 → **@8.5**. You held the *loads* correctly, which
  was the letter of the cap — but the cap existed because a steroid was muffling your pain signal, and
  an @8.5 backoff triple is the thing it was written to stop. Minor in isolation; worth naming.
- **Reverse Pec Deck: 0 of 4 sessions. Never performed.** It was one of this block's deliberate new
  rotations and it simply didn't happen. It was also programmed at **45–55 lb when your logged history
  on that machine is ~100 lb.** I'd bet the prescription is why it got skipped — a load that light
  isn't worth the setup. That's a hypothesis, not a fact; tell me if it's wrong. Either way: re-anchor
  it near 100 or cut it and pick a rear-delt movement you'll actually do.
- **Iso-Lateral Row: 1 of 4.** The block's own standing orders predicted D1 would run out of time
  ("if 60 min runs out, drop the last accessory") — and it did, except you dropped the row rather
  than the last item. Stop pretending D1 fits five exercises. Cut one in B5 by design.
- **Accessory loads were wrong all block** (the reason for this week's tooling work). Six were
  materially light vs your log: Leg Extension **90–100 → 140**, Reverse Pec Deck **45 → 100**,
  DB Shoulder Press **50 → 70**, Meadows Row **60 → 80**, Concentration Curl **25 → 43**,
  Incline DB Press **50 → 65**. Root cause was partly a name-mapping bug — several plan names didn't
  resolve to the Hevy templates you log under, so they read as untrained. **Incline DB Press was
  introduced in this block as "NEW" when you have 66 logged sessions of it.** Fixed now
  (`sheet-load-sync`, `exercise-name-mapping`); the sync runs every Saturday from here.
- **BW AMRAP is still not being captured** — logged as `0×6`, `0×6`, `1×12`. Third block running. It's
  prescribed every week and has never produced a usable number. Either log it properly or cut it.
