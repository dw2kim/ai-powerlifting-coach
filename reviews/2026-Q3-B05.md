### 3.2 Five movements ran that the plan never contained, and nothing flagged any of them

This is the finding of the review. Every one of these is in the log; none appears in any weekly
review, any brain file, or any session note:

| Date | Ran | Should have been |
|---|---|---|
| 08-11 (W1-D2) | **Overhead Press (Barbell) 115×8, 6, 6, 6** | DB Shoulder Press 75 @7 |
| 08-17 (W2-D1) | **Upright Row (Barbell) 90×8 ×3** | not in the plan at all |
| 08-17, 08-24 (W2/W3-D1) | **Inverted Row ×3 sets** | not in the plan — and D1 is structurally barred from back work |
| 08-25 (W3-D2) | **T Bar Row 95×12 ×3** | not in the plan |
| 09-01 (W4-D2) | **Overhead Press (Barbell) 115×8 @9.5** | DB Shoulder Press 75 @7 |
| 09-16 (W6-D3) | **Incline Bench Press (Barbell) 185×4, 155×8 ×3** | Incline **DB** Press 60/hand @7 — explicitly dumbbell-only |

**The upright row is the one to take seriously.** Barbell upright row is about the most reliably
impingement-provocative movement in a commercial gym: it drives internal rotation and abduction
together, closing the subacromial space — the exact space where your bursitis and AC degeneration
live. It ran three sets at 90 lb, five weeks ago, and neither of us knew until today. I'm not going
to scold you for it — **the system's job was to catch that and the system didn't have an eye there.**
That's my gap, not yours.

**The mechanism, and it's the same one that bit the Seated Calf Raise:** every check in this repo
reads *loads for exercises the plan already names*. `reconcile_loads` sweeps prescribed exercises.
`block_report` tracks the Big 5. The weekly review compares planned days to logged days. **Nothing
reads the set of exercises in a session and asks "is this movement supposed to be here?"** A
substitution is invisible to all of it. That's a concrete, fixable hole and it's action item #1.

The W6-D3 barbell incline deserves a specific note: that session had a whole reconciliation written
about it, ending in *dumbbells only, 60/hand, hard @7* — and it ran as a barbell at 185. The RPE
stayed under cap, so the harm is near zero. The lesson isn't the harm, it's that **the most
carefully-argued prescription in the block was the one that ran as a different movement.**
